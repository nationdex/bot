# Contributing and Volunteering

My grattitude to those who seek to contribute and volunteer themselves for Project Nationdex 2026 is incredibly immense, and you have no idea how much it flatters my heart to see Nationdex being valued by you at some point. Thank you for your dedicated assistance to make the dex community blue and united again.

Now, allow us to inform you about the requirements to operate on the Project Nationdex 2026 and assist on its development.

## Prerequisites

Before anything, please make sure to install Node.js through their official [website](https://nodejs.org/), and then, follow the instructions from the [readme](https://github.com/nationdex/bot#readme) file to setup the codebase and test it on your environment.

## Recommended IDEs

- Visual Studio Code
- Antigravity

## Recommended Extensions

- ForgeVSC
- NPM Intellisense
- Biome
